# Social media playbook (Online Communications board)

## Approval rule (until January 2027)
Use the shared sheet made from [approval-sheet-template.csv](approval-sheet-template.csv).
- **Posts and replies:** 2 of the 3 Online Communications board members approve. The author cannot be one of the two.
- **Announcements** (concert, Mass changes, appeals): also the parish priest.
- **Any edit clears the approvals.** Approve the exact text and image.
- An emoji reaction in WhatsApp is not an approval; write your name in the sheet.
- Keep the sheet to content and approvals: no names, phone numbers or emails of members, enquirers or children.
- Limit access to the Online Communications board and the parish priest.

Review in early January: if the sheet was painful, compare ready-made tools (Postiz, Mixpost) before building one.

## What we never do
- Post photos or videos of identifiable singers without their consent; children only with written parental consent.
- Call ourselves "the Basilica Choir", or use the Basilica's logo or domain without permission.
- Buy followers, use fake accounts, send mass messages, or auto-reply as the choir.
- Promise guaranteed results, or post invented quotes, endorsements or performances.
- Ask for money through the choir; donations go only through the parish or a charity's own page.

## Rhythm
- Mid-October to mid-November: **one post a day** plus stories; the focus is recruiting (deadline Sat 24 Oct),
  then "bring a friend".
- Mid-November to the concert: countdown posts, rehearsal moments (with consent), invitations to reserve.
- Concert week: up to **three posts a day**.
- Every Friday: type follower numbers into Community → This week → Friday numbers.

## Share links that tell us what works
Add `?src=` to links so the welcome team can see where enquiries came from, without tracking anyone:
- Instagram: `https://<address>/?src=instagram#/join`
- Facebook: `https://<address>/?src=facebook#/join`
- WhatsApp: `https://<address>/?src=whatsapp#/join`
- QR codes on cards: `?src=qr` (the QR codes in the app already include it)

## Using AI for drafts
AI (Claude) may draft captions, replies, translations and ideas. People decide. Check every fact (dates, times,
places), keep our name right, and never let AI invent quotes or numbers.

## Measuring honestly
Compare with the starting numbers taken in October: followers on each platform, enquiries, first rehearsals,
new members, concert reservations and headcount, and donations reported by the parish or charity. Report what
actually happened, including what didn't work.
