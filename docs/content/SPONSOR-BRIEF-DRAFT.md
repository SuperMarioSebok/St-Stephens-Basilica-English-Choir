# Supporting the English Choir of St. Stephen's Basilica (draft for review)

*Draft only. Not to be sent until the parish priest has approved it and the parish has confirmed how support is
received. The choir itself handles no money.*

## Who we are
The English Choir of St. Stephen's Basilica sings at the English Mass in St. Stephen's Basilica, Budapest. Our
singers come from many countries. Our purpose is simple: *sing praises to God, in St. Stephen's and all over
the world.*

## What we are doing this year
- Our **Christmas Concert on [DATE]** closes the 10th anniversary of the English-speaking community of the Basilica.
- We are welcoming new singers and volunteers.
- Volunteers have built a free phone app that helps every singer practise their part at home.

## How you can help
| Way to help | What it makes possible |
|---|---|
| **Come and bring others** to the Christmas Concert | A full church and a shared celebration |
| **Share our invitations** with your community or staff | More singers, volunteers and listeners |
| **Give your skills**: video, photography, design, translation, IT | Better invitations and practice materials |
| **Lend equipment**: a recording microphone, a keyboard, music stands | Better rehearsals and practice recordings |
| **Donate through [the parish / the charity the choir supports]** | [What the parish or charity uses it for] |

We do not offer advertising in exchange for support. We are glad to thank supporters publicly, with their
permission.
