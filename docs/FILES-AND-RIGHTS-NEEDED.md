# Files, rights and decisions needed before real repertoire

No real choir arrangement has been created, guessed or substituted. A title alone ("Sanctus", "Gloria") does not
identify a composer, arrangement or edition, so nothing will be loaded until the items below arrive.

## For each piece (first: Sanctus, Ndikhokhele Bawo, Gloria)

| # | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Exact identification**: composer, arranger, edition/publisher, voicing (SATB, SSA, divisi?), language | Avoids loading a similarly named but different arrangement |
| 2 | **Best source file**, in this order of preference: MusicXML/MXL from the publisher or a notation program (MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale, Dorico) → the original notation file → a clean PDF/scan (Phase 2, needs OMR and manual correction) | MusicXML gives exact notes and bars; scans need human correction |
| 3 | **The printed copy singers use** (PDF or photo of the first page is enough) | Bar numbers and part names must match rehearsal |
| 4 | **Written permission** (email is fine) for member-only practice use: who owns the arrangement, what is allowed (making practice audio, showing the score in a private app), for how long | Copyright; recorded in the song's rights note |
| 5 | Intended **tempo** and how to treat **repeats/endings** | Repeats are not expanded automatically |
| 6 | Who **confirms the mapping** and listens per part (the four voice leads) | Approval gate |
| 7 | For the fallback route only: an **authorized recording** + who recorded it + permission | Recording rights are separate from score rights |
